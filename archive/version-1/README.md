# Version 1 (July 2026), superseded

This folder holds version 1 of this work as it was published in July 2026, under the title *Mirror-Constrained Black Hole Thermodynamics*. It is kept unchanged for the record. It is superseded by the short article in `../../paper/`, and its known errors are listed in `../../ERRATA.md`. Please do not cite it.

- `paper/` : the manuscript (markdown source of record, LaTeX, PDF, 31 pages).
- `ancillary/` : the ancillary file.
- `code/` : the scripts of version 1, and a run log of all of them made on 2026-11-11.
- `review/` : the novelty review and the integration checklist of July 2026, kept as records.
- `drafts/` : working drafts of Sections 9 and 10.

The paths quoted inside these files are those of the time, when these folders were at the top level of the repository.
